# Credits

**Studio for DeepSeek Harness** is made by **RdotA**.

The same credits are in the app: **Studio → Credits**.

## Built on

[DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness) by DeepSeek, released under the MIT licence. Studio plugs into its theme, slot and plugin systems. The whale logo that **Identity → Original** restores is DeepSeek's own mark.
